HISTORICAL CREDITS

Chronicles of Swarajya: The Divine Blade is an artistic homage to Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj (1630–1680), the legendary architect of Hindavi Swarajya, celebrated for pioneering naval defense, guerrilla mountain warfare (Ganimi Kava), and just governance across the Sahyadri ranges.

Features 75 progressive campaign levels across historic battle arenas: Pratapgad, Lal Mahal, and the sovereign crowning at Fort Raigad.
